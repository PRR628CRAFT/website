Hey everyone, PRR628 here. As many of you may know, [Pterodactyl](https://pterodactyl.io) has... gone downhill. Pterodactyl is a hosting panel, primarily for Minecraft servers. There has been some drama due to a rebrand done by the parent company, [Infraly](https://infraly.co). Many people are rushing to find alternatives, however, it is not clear which one to pick. There are dozens of alternatives, each with their pros and cons. But no one source is really dedicated to compare and contrast all of the alternatives! That is why I am here. This post will be dedicated to giving you what you need to choose the next best panel for you.

### What is Pterodactyl
